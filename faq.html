Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ordering process work?

You submit a request through Start Your Order with details about what you need. We review it, confirm we can produce it, and send you accurate pricing and a timeline. Your order is confirmed once your invoice is paid — submitting a request does not confirm your order.

Do you do free design work or mockups?

Not before payment. Basic layout is included in the price of standard products. More involved custom design work carries an additional design fee, and complex logo or brand identity work may be referred to Dean Design Studio.

What's a proof, and how does approval work?

When your order requires design work, we send a watermarked digital proof for your approval before production starts. One standard revision is included where specified on your product; additional revisions may carry a fee. Print-ready artwork isn't provided unless it's specifically purchased or agreed to.

How do I pay?

We invoice through Square. Custom orders are paid in full before design or production begins — payment is what moves your request from quote to confirmed order.

How long does an order take?

Turnaround varies by product. Storefront door decals, for example, typically run 7–10 business days after payment and proof approval. Turnaround starts once your order is confirmed and approved, not when you first submit your request.

Can I get a rush order?

Rush orders are subject to availability and additional fees. Let us know your date needed when you submit your request and we'll tell you what's possible.

What if I already have my own artwork?

Great — upload it with your request. We'll confirm it's print-ready and let you know if anything needs adjusting before we quote.

What if my project isn't listed on your services page?

Use the Custom Quote option on Start Your Order. We only quote projects that fit our production capabilities, timeline and cost — if something's outside that, we'll let you know rather than promise it can be done.
